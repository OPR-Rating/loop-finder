Loop Finder

Tell it what you're struggling with. It finds the circle you're stuck in, shows it simply or in detail, and points to where a small push changes the most.

Anything at all: money, school, work, family, health, sport, confidence. Write it the way you'd tell a friend.

Where the answers come from

Four sources, one map

Coming first

AI, with a systems recipe

The app sends your words plus a fixed recipe: find the loops, name the pattern, rank the leverage points. The AI returns a map the app draws.

Next

Trusted content

Guides, videos and checklists from qualified teachers, coaches and advisers. The map links to the one that fits your loop.

Next

Live facts

A web search for things that change: house prices, deposit rules, exam dates. Shown with their sources, never guessed.

Later

Your own results

Check-ins, marks, savings or match scores. The app sees whether the loop is breaking and adjusts the next step.

Prototype. Maps are general guidance for thinking things through, not financial, medical or professional advice.
