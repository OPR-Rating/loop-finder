Loop Finder

Tell it what you're struggling with. Find the circle you're stuck in, see it simply or in detail, and spot where a small push changes the most. Let the app map it for you, or think it through yourself, step by step.

Before you start

The app reads what you write and draws your loop in about a minute.

Anything at all: money, school, work, family, health, sport, confidence. Write it the way you'd tell a friend.

Live now

AI, with a systems recipe

The app sends your words plus a fixed recipe: find the loops, name the pattern, rank the leverage points. The AI returns a map the app draws.

Next

Trusted content

Guides, videos and checklists from qualified teachers, coaches and advisers. The map links to the one that fits your loop.

Next

Live facts

A web search for things that change: house prices, deposit rules, exam dates. Shown with their sources, never guessed.

Later

Your own results

Check-ins, marks, savings or match scores. The app sees whether the loop is breaking and adjusts the next step.

Important

Loop Finder is a self-reflection tool. It is not therapy, counselling, or medical, psychological, financial, legal or career advice, and the people behind it are not psychologists, doctors or licensed advisers. Maps are general ideas to help you think. Decide for yourself what is right for you, and speak to a qualified professional before making important decisions.

If you are in distress or danger, contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline straight away, or talk to someone you trust.

Your words: in "Map it for me", what you type is sent to an AI service to draw your map; Loop Finder does not keep it. Don't include names, ID numbers or other private details. In "Think it through myself", nothing leaves your phone unless you choose "Go deeper with the coach", which sends your loop and answers to the AI coach; Loop Finder does not keep them either.

Prototype, offered as is. Use at your own discretion.
